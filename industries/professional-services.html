Problems We Solve

The overhead that
drains billable capacity.

Intake

Client intake is inconsistent and slow

New client onboarding depends on whoever handles the intake. Information is collected inconsistently, conflict checks happen manually if at all, and the engagement can't begin until someone assembles a complete file — which takes longer than it should.

→ Structured intake workflow with automated data collection, conflict checks, and file assembly
Proposals

Proposals and engagement letters take too long

Every proposal starts from scratch or from a template someone has to find and customize. Engagement letters require manual drafting for each matter. Work that could take minutes takes hours, and opportunities go cold while documents are being prepared.

→ AI-assisted proposal and engagement letter generation from structured intake data
Deadlines

Deadlines tracked in too many places

Statute of limitations dates, filing deadlines, response windows, and client deliverable dates are spread across calendars, practice management systems, and individual notes. A missed deadline in a law firm isn't an inconvenience — it's a malpractice claim.

→ Centralized deadline tracking with automated alerts and escalation protocols
Billing

Billing leakage from time capture gaps

Time entries logged at the end of the day or week are incomplete. Work gets done and never billed. Write-offs happen because invoices go out late and clients push back on charges they don't remember. Revenue walks out the door quietly.

→ Automated time capture prompts, billing reminders, and invoice generation workflows
In professional services, time is the product. Every hour your team spends on administrative work is an hour that doesn't bill — and a client who doesn't get the attention they're paying for.

Got Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does AI automation mean for a small law firm or professional services firm?

AI automation means replacing the administrative and process-heavy tasks that pull your team away from billable work with systems that handle them automatically. That includes client intake, conflict checks, billing reminders, deadline tracking, matter status updates, and document requests. For firms with 5 to 30 staff, those systems typically save 10 to 15 hours per person per week.

How much time can attorneys and paralegals actually save with AI?

Most attorneys and paralegals at the firms we work with recover 10 to 15 hours per week after full implementation. The biggest gains come from automating client intake sequences, billing follow-ups, deadline reminders, and matter status communications. Your actual savings depend on your current workflow setup, which is why every engagement starts with a diagnostic before we recommend anything.

How much does AI automation cost for a small law firm or professional services firm?

There is no flat rate because every firm operates differently. We scope every engagement to your team size and workflow complexity before quoting anything. The best starting point is the free AI Audit, which maps your operations and identifies where automation delivers the most value before you spend a dollar.

Is AI automation compliant with legal ethics rules and professional responsibility requirements?

Yes. Every system we build is designed compliance-first with professional responsibility rules and client confidentiality requirements built in from the start. We account for bar association guidelines on technology use, client data handling, and matter documentation before any automation goes live. All workflows are fully documented so you have a clear record if a bar inquiry or client audit ever arises.

How long does AI implementation take for a law firm or professional services firm?

Most firms see their first automations live within 4 to 6 weeks of kickoff. We start with process documentation and a prioritized roadmap so every build is justified before it is built, then roll out in phases so your team's client work is never disrupted. Full implementation across intake, billing, and matter management workflows typically runs 8 to 14 weeks.

What happens during the free AI Audit?

The free AI Audit is a 60-minute working session where we walk through your current operations and identify every workflow ready for automation. We look at client intake, billing processes, deadline management, and matter communications. Within 48 hours you receive a written report with a prioritized list of automation opportunities and estimated time savings. There is no cost and no obligation to continue.
