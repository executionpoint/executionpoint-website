Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a typical staffing agency or freelance platform?

We do not source from databases or post job listings. Every engineer in our network has been evaluated firsthand on real technical work by our team, who has worked alongside them in production environments. We also review every placement against your industry's compliance requirements and put proper contracts, data handling agreements, and access controls in place before the first working session. Staffing agencies screen resumes. We vouch for the people we place.

What roles can you place?

We place AI engineers who build and fine-tune AI agents and LLM integrations; automation developers who handle workflow automation, API integrations, and no-code and low-code implementations; data engineers who design and structure pipelines for AI readiness; backend developers who support AI system infrastructure; and QA and testing specialists for validation, edge case testing, and compliance verification.

How quickly can you place someone?

Most placements are matched and onboarded within days, not months. Because we work from an existing vetted network rather than starting a search from scratch, we can move quickly when the right fit is available. Timeline depends on the specificity of your technical requirements and compliance needs.

Do your engineers work US business hours?

Yes. Our network operates within US Eastern and Central time zones. There is no 8-hour lag or async-only workflow. Your augmented team member attends standups, responds in real time, and collaborates the same way an in-house hire would.

How much does staff augmentation cost compared to US-based hiring?

Small businesses typically cut AI delivery costs by 40 to 60 percent compared to equivalent US-based hiring. Exact rates depend on the role, seniority, and engagement length. We scope every placement individually. Start with the free AI Audit so we can understand your technical requirements before quoting anything.
