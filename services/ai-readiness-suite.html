Frequently Asked Questions

What is the AI Readiness Suite and what does it include?

The AI Readiness Suite is a set of three assessments Execution Point Consulting runs before any implementation begins. The first is the AI and Automation Audit, a 60 to 90 minute operational walkthrough that maps your workflows and quantifies time and cost savings. The second is the Data Readiness Assessment, which audits your data sources, quality, and structure across your current tools. The third is the Security and Compliance Assessment, which identifies sensitive data types, maps applicable regulations, and flags current risk exposure.

Do I have to buy all three assessments together?

No. You can start with just the AI and Automation Audit, which is free. Most clients begin there and add the Data Readiness and Compliance assessments once they have seen the opportunity map. All three together give you the full picture needed to make a confident implementation decision.

How quickly do we receive the results?

You receive a written report within 48 hours of each assessment session. The report includes a prioritized opportunity map with quantified ROI for the AI Audit, a data readiness score with gap analysis for the Data Assessment, and a security gap report with implementation recommendations for the Compliance Assessment.

Do we have to move forward with implementation after the assessments?

No. There is no obligation to continue after any assessment. You keep the reports either way. Many clients use the output to inform internal planning or evaluate other vendors. If you do choose to build with us, the assessment work feeds directly into the implementation design so nothing is duplicated.

Who should go through the AI Readiness Suite?

Any small or mid-sized business that handles regulated data, operates across multiple software tools, or has team members spending significant time on repetitive manual work. The suite is especially valuable for real estate brokerages, insurance agencies, and professional services firms where compliance and data quality directly affect what can be automated.
