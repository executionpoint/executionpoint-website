Frequently Asked Questions

What does the AI Operations Retainer actually cover each month?

Every retainer month includes six deliverables: a performance review of all active AI systems against agreed KPIs; continuous system optimization as your workflows and tools evolve; proactive identification of new automation opportunities with ROI estimates; priority support with same-day acknowledgment and critical issues addressed within hours; compliance monitoring for regulatory changes relevant to your industry; and a written monthly summary report you can share with your leadership team.

When does it make sense to start a retainer?

The retainer is designed to follow an implementation engagement. AI systems built and left alone lose value over time. Models drift, workflows change, and new opportunities appear. The retainer is what keeps your automation investment compounding instead of degrading. Most clients start it immediately after their first implementation goes live.

Can you take over operations for AI systems another vendor built?

Yes. We can take over operations and optimization for AI systems built by other vendors after completing a technical review of what was built. We need to understand the architecture and documentation before committing to ongoing support. If the system is well-documented and built on standard commercial tools, onboarding is straightforward.

Is there a long-term contract commitment?

No. The retainer runs month to month and can be cancelled with 30 days notice. There is no annual lock-in. We earn your continued business every month based on the value we deliver.

How much does the AI Operations Retainer cost?

Retainer pricing depends on the number of active systems, complexity, and the level of support your operations require. Every retainer is scoped after implementation so the price reflects what is actually being maintained.
