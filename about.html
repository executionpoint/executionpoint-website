Our Story

Why Execution Point exists.

After 20 years in enterprise software and B2B sales, I kept seeing the same pattern: powerful AI tools being sold to small businesses with no real implementation plan, no compliance guardrails, and no knowledge transfer.

The business owner was left holding technology they didn't fully understand and couldn't maintain. Execution Point exists to fix that.
