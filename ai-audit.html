What the Audit Covers

  • Workflow Mapping

    We walk through how your team spends its time day to day and document every recurring process. This gives us a clear picture of where time is going before we recommend anything.

  • Automation Opportunity Identification

    We flag every workflow step that can be automated and estimate the time savings for each one. Most clients uncover 10 to 15 hours of automatable work per person per week.

  • Compliance Review

    We identify any regulatory or compliance considerations that affect how automation can be applied in your business. This is especially important for real estate, insurance, and legal workflows.

  • Prioritized Implementation Plan

    You receive a written report ranking your automation opportunities by impact and feasibility. The report tells you what to build first, what it will take, and what you can expect in time savings.

  • Tool and Platform Recommendations

    We recommend specific tools and platforms only after we understand your workflows and existing systems. We are platform-agnostic, so recommendations are based on fit, not vendor preference.

Who It Is For

  • Independent real estate brokerages with 10 to 75 agents looking to automate lead handling, transaction coordination, compliance tracking, and agent onboarding.
  • Insurance agencies with 5 to 50 staff looking to automate policy renewals, claims intake, carrier submissions, and E&O documentation.
  • Professional services firms and law firms with 5 to 30 staff looking to automate client intake, billing workflows, deadline tracking, and matter communications.
