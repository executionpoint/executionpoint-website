Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do I get from the free AI Audit?

You receive a written report delivered within 48 hours of your discovery call. It includes your AI readiness score, the top process gaps identified in your operations, and a prioritized list of three to five quick-win automations you can implement within 90 days. The report also flags any compliance risks relevant to your industry before you build anything. It is yours to keep with no obligation to continue.

How long does the audit take?

The process takes two steps. First, you submit a short form describing your business and your biggest operational challenge. We review your answers before the call so we come prepared. Second, we schedule a focused 30-minute discovery call to ask follow-up questions and walk through your current workflows. Your written report arrives within 48 hours of that call.

Is there any cost or commitment?

No. The audit is completely free. There is no sales pressure, no obligation to engage further, and no requirement to share sensitive business data. You keep the report either way. If you choose to move forward with implementation or strategy work, that conversation happens after you have seen the report and decided it is worth pursuing.

Who is the free AI Audit for?

It is designed for small and mid-sized businesses in real estate, insurance, professional services, or any industry with significant manual workflow volume. It is most useful if you suspect time is being lost to repetitive tasks but are not sure where to start, if you want an honest assessment before investing in AI, or if you need a written plan to bring to leadership or partners.

Is the audit available in Spanish?

Yes. Discovery calls and all written deliverables are available in English and Spanish. Bilingual consultations are offered at no additional cost.
