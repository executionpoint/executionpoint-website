Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need all seven services, or can I start with just one?

You can start with just one. Most clients begin with the free AI Audit, which is part of the AI Readiness Suite. From there, the path depends on what the audit surfaces. Some clients move straight to implementation. Others start with process documentation first. Every engagement is scoped to where you actually are, not a predetermined package.

What order should we go through the services?

The sequence that produces the best results is: assess first with the AI Readiness Suite, document your processes, build with AI and Automation Implementation, train your team with AI Training and Enablement, then scale with the Operations Retainer. You do not have to follow every step, but skipping assessment and documentation before building is the most common reason AI projects underdeliver.

How is Execution Point Consulting different from a software vendor or a large consultancy?

We are an independent consulting firm, not a software vendor. We are platform-agnostic and have no financial relationship with any tool we recommend. We also work exclusively with small and mid-sized businesses, which means our engagements are scoped and priced for SMBs, not enterprise budgets. Every recommendation is built around your actual operations, not a predetermined framework.

What industries do you work with?

We work primarily with real estate brokerages, independent insurance agencies, and professional services firms including law firms. These industries share common characteristics: regulated data, significant manual workflow volume, and clear automation opportunities that deliver measurable ROI. We also work with SMBs in adjacent industries on a case-by-case basis.

Where do I start if I have no idea what I need?

Start with the free AI Audit. It is a 60 to 90 minute working session where we map your operations, identify every automation opportunity, and deliver a written report with quantified ROI within 48 hours. No cost, no commitment. The report tells you exactly what to do next, whether that is with us or on your own.
